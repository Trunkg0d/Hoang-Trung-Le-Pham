About me

I am a teaching assistant at the University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM and a researcher with the IEC Research Group. I am also completing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Science, VNU-HCM.

My research focuses on machine unlearning and model security. I study attacks against machine learning systems and methods for evaluating whether an unlearned model has removed the influence of selected data.

Research interests

  • Machine unlearning: unlearning methods, removal quality, and privacy evaluation.
  • Model security: inversion attacks, dropout attacks, bit-flip attacks, and knowledge editing.
  • Multimodal learning: graph, textual, interaction, and temporal information for rumor detection.
