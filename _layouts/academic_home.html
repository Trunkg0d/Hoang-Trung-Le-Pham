About me

I am a teaching assistant at the University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM and a researcher with the IEC Research Group (UIT, VNU-HCM). I obtained a B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Science, VNU-HCM, with an Excellent classification, under the supervision of Prof. Kim-Hung Le and Lecturer Anh-Duy Tran. I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at the same university under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Kim-Hung Le and Dr. Hung Phuoc Truong.

My research focuses on multimodal learning and AI model security (e.g., layer attacks, inversion attacks). Currently, I investigate inversion attacks against machine learning systems, evaluate whether an unlearned model has removed the influence of selected data, and study knowledge-editing attacks on multimodal models.

Research interests

  • Machine unlearning: unlearning methods, removal quality, and privacy evaluation.
  • Model security: inversion attacks, dropout attacks, bit-flip attacks, and knowledge editing.
  • Multimodal learning: graph, textual, interaction, and temporal information for rumor detection.
