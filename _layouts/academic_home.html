About me

Building machine learning systems that remain trustworthy when models learn, forget, and change.

I am a researcher and teaching assistant at the University of Information Technology, VNU-HCM, and a member of the IEC Research Group. My work focuses on the security and privacy of machine learning systems, especially machine unlearning and attacks that reveal what a model was meant to forget.

I am currently completing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Science, VNU-HCM. I also explore multimodal learning and practical AI systems.
